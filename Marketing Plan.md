## 2. When we launch

Hakbang launches **twice**. First comes a small, closed pilot that proves the model. Then comes the public launch, which uses the pilot's results as proof.

| Date | Milestone | What it means for marketing |
|---|---|---|
| Nov 27 – Dec 1, 2026 | Course prototype and defense | The MVP demo is the first public look at the brand |
| **by Jan 8, 2027** | 20 interviews done, fee tested | Confirms the message, the ₱5,000 fee and the 70% hire target |
| **Jan 29, 2027** | First pilot partner signs a letter of intent | The first employer is in. Nothing goes to job seekers before this. |
| Mar 29 – Apr 5, 2027 | Pilot release goes live | CMO opens sign-ups **to the pilot cohort only**: DLSU plus 2–3 nearby universities |
| **Apr 5, 2027** | **Soft launch** | The first partner's track is open. 20–30 trainees. |
| May 2027 | First hires | The first "Hakbang Stories" can be told, with each trainee's consent |
| **Jun 30, 2027** | Pilot results report | Hire rate, completion and retention measured. This becomes the sales proof. |
| **Aug 2027** | **Public launch** (launch release) | #UnangHakbang goes live. Endorsements, the Skill-Gap Check, reminders and referral codes are switched on. About 10 Metro Manila schools. |
| Dec 2027 | Go/no-go review | At least 3 active partners and about 319 hires. Decides the mobile app and the seed round. |
| Feb 2028 | Seed round closes | ₱150,000 of it goes to marketing: #UnangHakbang 2028 and outreach to underemployed young workers (Ch. X Table 10.3) |

**The one rule that sets the order:** employers come first. *"A job seeker who signs up and finds no open training tracks will not come back"* (Ch. IV A.5). Sign-ups open only after a partner's track is live.

## 3. How we know we're ready

Each launch has a gate. If a gate isn't met, we don't move forward. We fix the problem or use the fallback.

### Gate 1: Ready to build (by January 2027)
From Ch. II D.3, Phase 1 → 2.
- [ ] 20 interviews done: 12 job seekers, 6 employers, 2 partners
- [ ] Interviews **confirm the main pain points**: "no experience" rejections, scam fears, and training money lost to dropouts
- [ ] The ₱5,000 fee was tested, and confirmed or changed
- [ ] **At least a few employers agree to a pilot**, and one signs a letter of intent by Jan 29

### Gate 2: Ready for soft launch (April 5, 2027)
**Product.** "Done" is defined in Ch. V A.4.
- [ ] One partner's track runs **end to end** in the app, from match to hire
- [ ] The verification checklist and the privacy and consent notice are live
- [ ] Usability tests with 10–15 recent grads pass, and the security check is closed (Mar 28)
- [ ] Cloud services are on paid plans, since free tiers don't allow commercial use (Ch. V B.1)

**Employer side**
- [ ] At least one verified partner has signed the partner agreement: track, slots, hiring commitment, fee, data rules (Ch. IV D.1)
- [ ] That partner's track is **live in the app before sign-ups open**. The COO enters it for them if needed (Ch. V Table 5.6).

**Legal.** All of these happen before the pilot (Ch. VI Table 6.7).
- [ ] SEC registration, BIR registration and the business permit, so we can sign and invoice
- [ ] Asked DOLE whether a recruitment license applies
- [ ] NPC registration and a named Data Protection Officer
- [ ] Legal review of the privacy policy, terms and partner agreement

**Marketing**
- [ ] Pilot cohort channels are ready: career offices at DLSU and 2–3 nearby schools, plus graduate Facebook groups
- [ ] Trust markers are on every track: "Verified Employer" badge, written hiring commitment, clear terms (Ch. IV A.4)
- [ ] Help desk and report button are live: 24-hour replies, 48-hour scam review (Ch. IV D.2)

### Gate 3: Ready for public launch (August 2027)
**Proof from the pilot.** The Jun 30 report measures these (Ch. II D.4):

| Measure | Target | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Hire rate (trainees who pass and are hired) | **At least 70%** | Ch. IV Table 4.4 |
| Training completion rate | Set after the interviews **(proposed: agree on a target before the pilot starts)** | Measured in Ch. II D.4 |
| Three-month retention of hires | Reported to the employer next to its old numbers | Ch. II D.4, Ch. IV C.3 |
| Employer screening time saved | Reported to the employer | Ch. II D.4 |
| Feedback from both sides | Collected, and fixes made | Ch. II D.4 |

**Supply**
- [ ] **3–5 partners** signed or in a pilot, so there are enough tracks for about 10 schools (Ch. IV A.6)
- [ ] At least one pilot turned into a 12-month partner agreement (Ch. IV Table 4.4)

**Product.** The launch release (Ch. V Table 5.2).
- [ ] Verified Endorsements, the AI Skill-Gap Check, reminders and referral codes are live

**Marketing**
- [ ] At least one case study approved by its employer, and Hakbang Stories approved by the trainees (Ch. IV C.3)
- [ ] #UnangHakbang content, career office links and campus event dates locked for about 10 schools

**Money**
- [ ] Monthly fees cover monthly costs. The model expects this from July 2027 (Ch. IX E).

### Gate 4: The December 2027 go/no-go
- [ ] **At least 3 active employer partners and about 319 hires** (Ch. V Table 5.3)
- If met: build the mobile app in 2028 and raise the seed round.
- If not met: stay web-only and put the mobile budget into the core service (Ch. V Table 5.6).

### If a gate fails
| Problem | What we do | Source |
|---|---|---|
| No employer signs for the pilot | Widen outreach to MSMEs with 50+ workers | Ch. V Table 5.6 |
| The fee is too high or too low | Adjust it to sit below what employers spend per hire today | Ch. IV B.3 |
| Matches are weak | The COO reviews every pilot match by hand while the engine improves | Ch. V Table 5.5 |
| DOLE says we need a license | Position Hakbang as a software tool where the employer makes every hiring decision, or partner with a licensed agency | Ch. V Table 5.6 |
| A fake employer gets through | Suspend them, move the affected trainees to other tracks, and report to DOLE | Ch. V Table 5.6 |

## 4. Who we're marketing to

**Segments** (Ch. IV A.1). Job seekers: fresh college grads, underemployed young workers, SHS and TESDA graduates, informal workers, career shifters. Employers: IT-BPM firms and GCCs, retail and logistics, MSMEs.

**Beachhead** (Ch. II D.1). Fresh grads in Metro Manila matched to entry-level IT-BPM roles. This segment scored **24 out of 25**: easy for us to reach, strong employer demand, already job hunting on their phones, and word of mouth spreads within a batch.

| Persona | Who | What they need to hear |
|---|---|---|
| **Paolo** | BSBA grad, 8 months job hunting, part-time at a mall | "You don't need experience. You need to finish the training." |
| **Bea** | Did a "free training" with no job offer, now distrusts job posts | "Every company is verified, and the hiring promise is in writing." |
| **Marco** | 4th-year IT student, graduating in 4 months | "Start your profile now. Get matched the week you graduate." |
| **Carla** | BPO training lead filling 40–60 seats a month | "Pre-matched trainees, and you pay only for hires." |

**Who they listen to** (Ch. III Tables 3.3, 3.6). Job seekers take advice from batchmates, older siblings, school career offices and Facebook job groups. On the employer side, the decision sits with the talent acquisition, HR or L&D lead.

## 5. Positioning and messaging

**Positioning** (Ch. IV A.3)
- **For job seekers:** for fresh graduates who keep hearing "no experience," Hakbang is a train-to-hire hub that links them to verified employers who train them and commit to hiring those who pass. A job board ends at the listing and a course site ends at the certificate. Hakbang follows the trainee to a signed contract.
- **For employers:** for HR and L&D managers who lose training money on people who quit, Hakbang sends pre-screened, pre-matched trainees, and the company pays only when a trainee is hired.

**Message pillars**

| Job seekers | Employers |
|---|---|
| **Legit.** Every employer is verified. | **Pay for results.** No hire, no fee. |
| **A job at the end.** The hiring commitment is written down before you apply. | **Pre-matched.** A shortlist, not hundreds of applications. |
| **Free.** Always, at every stage. | **Lower risk.** 90-day replacement guarantee. |
| **Your effort counts.** Endorsements stay on your profile even if you're not hired. | **Visible.** Every trainee's progress in one dashboard. |

**Brand** (Ch. IV A.4)
- Name: "Hakbang" means "step." Tagline: *Isang hakbang papunta sa trabaho.*
- Voice: friendly Taglish for job seekers on social media; plain, data-backed English for employers.
- Trust markers: the "Verified Employer" badge, hire counts on each track, and real hire stories shared with consent.
- **Don't** use "Grab for train-to-hire" in public marketing. It uses another company's brand, so it's for class and internal use only (Ch. V E).

## 7. Pricing

| Item | Who pays | Price |
|---|---|---|
| Account, profile, matching, training | Job seeker | **Free, always** |
| Standard success fee | Employer | ₱5,000 per hire |
| Pilot rate | Employer | ₱2,500 per hire, first batch only |
| Volume rate | Employer | ₱4,000 per hire, for 30+ hires a quarter |
| Employer Pro dashboard | Employer | ₱10,000 a month, optional, from 2028 |

There's no setup or listing fee. Billing is monthly with 30-day terms. The fee only counts once the trainee signs an employment contract (Ch. IV B). **Talking point:** an agency charges 15–25% of first-year salary, which is about ₱32,400–₱54,000 for an ₱18,000-a-month role (Ch. III C.3).

## 8. Employer sales plan

**Owner:** CEO, with the COO and CFO. Year 1 sales are done by the founders (Ch. IV C.1).

| Stage | What we do | Target |
|---|---|---|
| Prospect | List BPOs, GCCs and mid-size firms from industry lists, LinkedIn and alumni contacts | 50 companies |
| Contact | LinkedIn messages and emails; warm introductions from faculty and alumni | 20 meetings |
| Discovery call | Ask about hiring volume, cost per hire and attrition in the first 3 months | 10 interested firms |
| Pilot proposal | One batch of 20 trainees at the pilot rate, with clear success measures | 3–5 pilots |
| Pilot | Run the track, with a weekly progress report to the employer | 70%+ of trainees hired |
| Contract | 12-month partner agreement at the standard or volume rate | 3–5 partners in Year 1 |

**How we close** (Ch. IV C.3): half-price pilot, a results report comparing cost per hire and 90-day retention with the employer's old numbers, case studies from earlier pilots, and the replacement guarantee. **Extra selling point:** companies that run their own training may get tax incentives under the EBET Act, RA 12063 (Ch. II D.5).

## 9. Job seeker acquisition

**Owner:** CMO, with the COO.

**Funnel:** Awareness → Sign up → Complete Living Profile → Matched to a track → Finish training → Hired (Ch. IV C.2)

| Stage | Tactic |
|---|---|
| Awareness | Campus career talks, career office referral links, student org partners, graduate Facebook groups, TikTok |
| Sign up | A basic Living Profile in **under 5 minutes** |
| Matched | Proof up front on every track: Verified Employer badge, written hiring commitment, past hire counts |
| Finish training | Messenger, SMS and email reminders; small batches so trainees push each other |
| Hired, and beyond | Batchmate referral code. With their consent, the trainee's story becomes a Hakbang Story. |

## 10. The #UnangHakbang campaign

*UnangHakbang* means "first step." It's our launch campaign, timed to **graduation season**, when our beachhead starts job hunting (Ch. IV E). It goes live with the **August 2027 public launch** and runs again in 2028 with seed funding (Ch. X Table 10.3).

**Content themes** (Ch. IV E)
1. **Interview tips:** practical, short, shareable.
2. **"No experience" myths:** what entry-level employers actually look for.
3. **How to spot a scam post:** this builds trust in Hakbang without overselling it.
4. **Hakbang Stories:** real trainees, with their consent, from the pilot onward.

**Channels and measures**

| Channel | Audience | What we do | Measure |
|---|---|---|---|
| TikTok and Facebook | Job seekers | Short videos on the four themes | Sign-ups per post |
| Campus events | Job seekers | Career talks and job fair booths at DLSU and partner schools, co-hosted with student orgs | Sign-ups per event |
| Career office partners | Job seekers | Referral link in career office emails and pages; joint resume and interview workshops | Sign-ups per school |
| Micro-influencers | Job seekers | Career and BPO creators with 10K–100K followers, **paid per sign-up** | Cost per sign-up |
| Referral program | Job seekers | Bring a batchmate and get a free mock interview with an HR volunteer once they finish training | Referrals per user |
| LinkedIn and industry events | Employers | Posts with pilot results; attend IT-BPM and HR events | Meetings booked |
| Public relations | Both | Pitch to campus papers and business news after our **first 100 hires**; startup contests and incubators like DLSU Animo Labs | Articles and inbound leads |

*(Ch. IV Table 4.5)*
